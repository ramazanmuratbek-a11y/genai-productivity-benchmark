# Illustrative task sets — to be pilot-tested before any study

Both task sets require a **180–220-word internal business update** with a subject,
short summary, three bullet-point recommendations, and a professional tone.
Only the supplied facts may be used. Both have five verifiable anchor facts.

## Set A — Customer operations

**Scenario:** Write an update to the operations director summarizing an internal
support pilot, its constraints, and actions to take next.

**Source facts:**
1. Pilot period: 1–14 September (14 days).
2. Team size: 12 support agents.
3. Tickets resolved: 480.
4. Average first response time: 3 hours.
5. Follow-up audit found 18 incorrectly categorized tickets.

**Deliverable:** Identify one operational risk, note at least one limitation of
the pilot, and provide three justified next steps without inventing evidence.

## Set B — Training operations

**Scenario:** Write an update to the department director summarizing an internal
training pilot, its constraints, and actions to take next.

**Source facts:**
1. Pilot period: 1–14 October (14 days).
2. Cohort size: 12 employees.
3. Training assignments completed: 480.
4. Average help-desk response time: 3 hours.
5. Follow-up audit found 18 incorrectly classified assignments.

**Deliverable:** Identify one operational risk, note at least one limitation of
the pilot, and provide three justified next steps without inventing evidence.

## Research design note
These are *illustrative* matched tasks, not validated equivalents. They require
pilot testing for difficulty and realism before they are used with participants.
The facts above are fictional task stimuli, **not empirical research outcomes**.
